Our Vision

Automotive Data, Reimagined.

CaruLogiX transforms Caruso’s automotive Data Catalog into a clearer, more visual and user-centered experience. Interactive data categories help users navigate large amounts of data more intuitively, while the featured package view highlights relevant data packages and supports actions such as comparing and combining existing packages or creating custom ones. Together with a 3D car view and a modernized interface, CaruLogiX simplifies the exploration, understanding, and use of complex automotive data.

About Us

We are Promptify, an interdisciplinary student team from the Technische Hochschule Mannheim. Our core team consists of five computer science students, supported by two design students who helped shape the visual direction and user experience of CaruLogiX.

This project was developed as part of our SEP module in the summer semester of 2026. Together, we combined software development, data understanding and design thinking to create an innovative, user-friendly solution for exploring Caruso’s automotive data catalog.
